# AI Shorts Bot Terms of Service

Effective date: 2026-09-26

AI Shorts Bot is a single-user automation for generating original short-form videos and uploading them to the operator's own YouTube channel.

## Permitted Use

The software is intended for lawful creator automation, including:

- generating original video content;
- rendering vertical short-form videos;
- preparing titles, descriptions, hashtags, and related metadata;
- uploading videos to the operator's own authorized YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3.

## Prohibited Use

The software must not be used to:

- artificially inflate views, likes, comments, subscribers, or watch time;
- purchase, exchange, or automate fake engagement;
- upload content the operator does not have the right to use;
- bypass YouTube platform restrictions, API quotas, compliance reviews, or security controls;
- impersonate another creator or access another user's YouTube account without authorization.

## YouTube and Google Terms

Use of YouTube functionality is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, YouTube API Services Terms of Service, YouTube API Services Developer Policies, and applicable Google policies.

## Account Authorization

The YouTube upload feature requires OAuth 2.0 authorization from the Google account that controls the target YouTube channel. Access can be revoked by the operator at any time.

## Content Responsibility

The operator is responsible for reviewing generated content, ensuring that it complies with YouTube Community Guidelines, copyright rules, disclosure requirements, and other applicable laws and platform policies.

## Availability

The software is provided as an automation prototype without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation. YouTube, Google, GitHub, or other service changes may affect its functionality.
