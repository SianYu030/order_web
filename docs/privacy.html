Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

AI Shorts Bot is a private, single-user workflow used only by its owner. It uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage original videos on the owner's YouTube channel.

Information accessed

The application may use OAuth 2.0 authorization data required to access the owner's YouTube account and perform authorized upload-related operations. It does not request passwords and does not provide access to third-party users.

How information is used

Authorized YouTube account data is used only to perform actions requested by the channel owner, such as uploading original videos and setting video metadata or privacy status.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials used by the private workflow are stored securely as repository secrets and are not intentionally exposed in public source code. The application does not sell personal information and does not share authorized YouTube account data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's Google account access at any time from Google's account security permissions page.

YouTube and Google policies

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to the primary contact identified in the YouTube API compliance submission.
